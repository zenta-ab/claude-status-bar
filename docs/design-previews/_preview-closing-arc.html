Closing Arc

The forecast is drawn as a ghost arc that continues where consumption has actually reached, running exactly as far as the model says you will burn before the window resets. So the unused remainder stops being decoration and becomes the answer: if any daylight is left in the ring, you make it to reset. If the arc closes the circle, you don't. The alarm is a shape, not a colour.

The two channels inside one arc

Length is the point estimate. The ghost sweeps r × t_reset percent of the circle — how much quota the model expects you to spend between now and the reset. Long ghost = hot burn and/or a long time still to go.

The ending is the confidence. How the ghost terminates is quantised from the forecast's probability of cut-off, not from its point estimate. Three endings, three different pixels:

  • Track tone remaining — headroom the model will vouch for. P(cutoff) < 0.35
  • A transparent void — headroom it cannot vouch for; the taskbar shows through. 0.35–0.85
  • Nothing; closed at 12 o'clock — no headroom. > 0.85, and the point estimate must close too.

Precedence rule: the ending always wins over the length. A TIGHT verdict keeps a 1 px void even when the point estimate overshoots; a SAFE verdict keeps 3 px of track. At 16 px the ghost's length is approximate — its ending is exact.

What the icon is saying

Inner disc — session. Neutral wedge to 201.6° = the 56% already spent. The red ghost continues from there and lands exactly on 12 o'clock. It wanted to sweep 71.3% (0.4097 %/min × 168 min) but only 44% of circle was left, so it is clipped — and clipping is precisely the alarm. No track tone survives anywhere in the disc. The circle is closed.

The bright rim arc (32 px only) sweeps the 27.3% that did not fit — 27.3 / 0.4097 = ~64 minutes dead in the water. That is the magnitude channel, and it exists only where there are pixels for it.

Outer ring — weekly. 61% spent, amber ghost projects +34.4% by Friday 07:00, and stops 4.6% short of closing. Amber-with-a-void, not red: the model's cut-off probability is around 0.55, so the ring says too close to call and shows the gap as taskbar, not as comfortable track.

The contrast worth noticing: a plain "56%" gauge and this icon show the same disc fill. Only one of them has already told you that you get cut off an hour before the window turns over, while the weekly is still undecided.

One invariant runs through both themes: distance from the taskbar colour is certainty. Track sits nearest the background, ghost in the middle, reality furthest away. The luminance order survives greyscale, so the icon still parses with all colour removed.

Rules the renderer must enforce

  • Never composite ghost alpha over the taskbar. A 45% orange over #f3f3f3 is a 1.15:1 ghost of a ghost. The "translucency" is baked into an opaque mid-tone per theme, so behaviour is identical on acrylic, solid, and wallpaper-through taskbars.
  • Minimum sweeps. Track wedge ≥ 3 px of visible arc at the element's outer edge, void ≥ 1 px, ghost ≥ 2 px or it is not drawn. Anything below the floor snaps to the floor, and the verdict tone carries the truth.
  • Closure is reserved. Only a DRY verdict may close the circle. TIGHT is clamped 1 px short, always. This is what makes the alarm colour-blind-safe: closed vs not-closed is pure geometry.
  • Hysteresis on the ending, not just the colour. 3 consecutive polls plus a 0.05 deadband on P(cutoff) before a terminal treatment changes, or the void will strobe open and shut at the boundary.
  • The now-slit is always drawn, in every state including 100% and including no-forecast. It is the datum that stops solid + ghost being read as one big fill.
  • Ship real assets at 16/20/24/32/40/48. Slit width, ring thickness and the overshoot rim are per-size decisions, not a scale factor. The overshoot rim and the soft band tail exist at 24 px and above only.
  • Rollover flushes to no-forecast for at least the hysteresis window. A ghost must never survive a reset boundary, and a re-seeded forecast must earn its colour back.
  • Tooltip carries what the pixels dropped: "Session 56% → 100% at 16:08, 64 min before reset (94%). Weekly 61% → ~95% at reset — too close to call."
